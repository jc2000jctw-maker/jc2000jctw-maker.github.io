Lighting, without the extra panel

Designed for quick, close-range fill light. Results depend on screen brightness and distance; a phone screen does not replace a large professional light.

How does selfie lighting work?

Open the app and adjust brightness and tone while checking the front camera preview. You can also place a second iPhone near your shooting camera as a separate light.

Can I use it during a video call?

Use a second iPhone beside your computer or calling device as an external light. The app does not keep a full-screen light visible behind another app on the same phone, and does not provide calling or livestreaming.

Can I light a small product?

Place the lit screen close to a small object and photograph it with another camera. Adjust the distance, brightness, and tone while watching shadows and reflections.
