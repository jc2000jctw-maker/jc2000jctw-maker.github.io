本機運算與權限

易經、八字與紫微排盤及解讀在手機本機運作。App 沒有自建帳號、廣告、追蹤或分析 SDK，也不把出生資料、解讀結果或感測資料傳送至開發者伺服器。出生資料由你自行輸入，不讀取通訊錄或裝置定位。鏡頭影像僅供即時辨識手勢，不錄影、不保存、不上傳，也不分析掌紋。動作感測資料僅在手機控制首頁星辰，不建立生物辨識資料庫。

保存、備份與刪除

未主動保存的出生資料與結果只在當次記憶體中使用；選擇「記住出生資料」或收藏時才寫入本機。出生盤收藏包含對應出生資料。設定會另保存語言及教學完成等偏好，並在本機保存已驗證的購買權限。你可刪除個別收藏，或在設定清除本機出生與收藏資料；語言與教學偏好另存。撤回鏡頭權限可至 iPhone 系統設定，仍可使用觸控。備份及備份移除依你的 Apple 裝置／iCloud 設定處理。

Apple 購買

Apple 依其政策處理試用啟用、付款及恢復購買。App 透過 StoreKit 驗證購買紀錄並在本機判定 7 天試用或永久使用權，不會取得你的 Apple 帳號密碼或付款卡資料，也不會為購買向 Apple 傳送出生資料、影像或動作樣本。啟用、購買與恢復購買需網路；排盤運算在本機進行。Apple 帳號與交易紀錄的保存／刪除由 Apple 管理，請參閱 Apple 隱私政策。

Privacy policy

Operator and scope

This policy covers the StellarYi iPhone app, operated by CHE-CHI CHUANG (莊哲奇). Privacy contact: jc2000jc.tw@gmail.com. Updated 18 October 2026.

On-device processing and permissions

I Ching, BaZi and Zi Wei charts and readings run on your phone. The app has no developer account system, ads, tracking or analytics SDK. It does not send birth details, readings, camera frames or motion samples to developer servers. You enter birth details yourself; the app does not read Contacts or device location. Camera frames are used only for live on-device hand gestures, without recording, storage, upload or palm-line analysis. Motion samples control the home star field locally; no biometric database is created.

Storage, backups and deletion

Unsaved birth details and readings are used in session memory. They are written locally only when you choose to remember birth details or save a reading; a saved birth reading includes its birth details. Language, tutorial completion and similar preferences are stored separately, as is locally verified purchase access. Delete individual readings or erase local birth and reading data in Settings; language and tutorial preferences remain separate. Revoke camera permission in iPhone Settings and continue with touch. Apple device/iCloud settings govern device backups and backup removal.

Apple purchases

Apple processes trial activation, payments and restoration under its own policies. StoreKit verifies purchases and the app determines seven-day trial or permanent access locally. The developer does not receive your Apple Account password or payment card details. Birth details, camera frames and motion samples are not sent to Apple for purchases. Activation, purchase and restore need a network connection; chart calculation is local. Apple manages retention and deletion of Apple account and transaction records; see Apple Privacy Policy.

Support and this website

If you choose to email support, we receive the email address and content you provide to answer and resolve your request. We delete correspondence no longer needed after resolution, except records that need to be retained. Email us to request deletion of support correspondence. Do not send passwords, payment information, camera recordings or unnecessary birth details. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; the host may process IP addresses and basic request logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages have no analytics scripts, ads or tracking cookies. The app does not attach chart details or camera images to the page URL.

Updates

Changes appear here with a revised date. Use the contact above for questions about local data, permissions or this policy.
